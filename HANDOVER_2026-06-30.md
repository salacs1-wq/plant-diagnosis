## Important API behavior

- Product-name usage search is strict by default.
- Related parallel / derived products are not mixed into product-name results unless:
  `include_related=true`
- Example:
  - `product_name=Sumi Alfa 5 EC` returns Sumi Alfa 5 EC records only.
  - `include_related=true` also allows related products such as Sumi-Alpha 050 EC or Wizard.
- Large usage responses support:
  - `limit`
  - `offset`
  - `has_more`
  - `next_offset`
